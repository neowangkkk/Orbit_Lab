Privacy Policy

A Legal Disclaimer

At ORBIT LAB, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. As a student-led initiative based at the University of Toronto, we collect and use limited information to support our research, events, and outreach activities. This policy outlines how we handle your data when you interact with us, whether through our website, event registration forms, mailing lists, or research participation.

What we collect

When you sign up for our newsletter, register for workshops, or participate in our research projects, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address, academic affiliation, or your responses to surveys or interviews. This information is used solely for the purpose of communicating with you about ORBIT LAB events and research opportunities, sharing relevant updates or publications, and improving the quality of our programs. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial use.

How we store it

Your information is stored securely and only accessible to authorized members of the ORBIT LAB team. We rely on trusted, password-protected platforms, such as University of Toronto email systems and institutional cloud services, to manage and protect this data. While we take reasonable precautions to keep your information safe, please be aware that no digital system is entirely immune to security risks.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time. If you wish to unsubscribe from our communications or have questions about how your data is being used, please contact us at info@orbitlab.ca. We may update this privacy policy from time to time, and any changes will be posted publicly. By continuing to engage with ORBIT LAB, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.
